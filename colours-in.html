Colours in the container

The container arrives with the colours you use most already in it — and each colour brings all five of its effects. Then pick your own colours, and drag the parts round inside.

1 · As it arrives

Five most-used colours, one wedge each. Every wedge carries that colour's five effects, from marker bleed on the outside to ink hatching at the heart.

the colours you use most are already in — each one brings all five of its effects

tap an effect to name it

2 · Picking your colours

Tap a cap to put that colour in the container; tap it again to take it out. The wheel re-divides itself to fit exactly what you chose — no empty slots.

tap a cap in, tap it again out — the wheel re-divides itself, no empty slots

tap a cap or an effect

3 · Drag them round

The parts sit round the inside of the container. Drag one to another place and they swap. Tap a part to see every effect it carries.

drag swaps places — a tap lists every effect the part carries

drag a part, or tap it to name it
