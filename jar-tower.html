The jar tower

Like the stacking pots in your picture: round jars screwed one on top of the other into a tower, each tier with its own colour-coded lid, a handle to carry the whole thing. Each tier holds one layer. To see what's in a tier, you turn its lid — drag it sideways like unscrewing — and it swings off, its contents spilling out beside the tower where you can reach them. The next lid is then ready. And one button pulls the whole tower apart into an exploded view.
