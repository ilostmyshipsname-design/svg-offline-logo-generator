The circle book, seen properly

Why it holds the most: pages can be added, and the book just re-divides itself. Here are four ways to see it — and at the end, a different kind of circle book whose pages fold through the spine, so it opens into a star and shows two faces per page.

1 · From above

The wheel book, looked straight down — the one from the count page.

the cover (watercolour wash) · twelve pages, one effect each · the spine hub (metallic sheen)

4 · The star book — a different kind

Pages folded through the spine instead of round it. Closed it's a plain circle; opened it stands as a star — and every page shows two faces, so it holds twice as much.

each leaf: front face (one effect) + back face (another effect) · twelve leaves = twenty-four holds
