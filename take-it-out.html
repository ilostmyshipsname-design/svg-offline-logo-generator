Take it out, put it back

This is what I should have built from the start: you open the container yourself, you drag a colour effect out of it and drop it where you work, and when you're done you drag it back and it packs into its place. No button unpacks it for you. Your hands do the unpacking — and the space is used exactly as much as the things you've taken out need.

1 · Open, drag out, drag back

Press open and the lid slides aside: the ten effects of one colour, packed in their places. Press one and drag — it follows your finger. Drop it on the paper and it stays out, in use. Drag it back over the container and let go: it packs into its slot.

2 · And what it's for: using it on the work

The same container over a paper with work on it. Drag an effect out and drop it onto a shape — the shape takes that colour effect. Drop it on empty paper and it just waits there, ready. Drag it back to pack it.
