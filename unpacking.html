Unpacking the circle

The parts live inside the circle. Press and they come out where you can use them; press again and they pack back in. Packed it takes little space; unpacked it is the working wheel. Same parts both times — nothing is added or thrown away.

1 · The spill

Twelve pages packed round a small ring. Spill them and they walk out into one straight row, full size and easy to reach. Pack them and they climb back into the ring.

packed: hard to read · spilled: every page full size — the ring is where they live, the row is where they work

tap a page to name it

2 · The lift

Eight layers sitting flat as one circle. Lift them and they stand up in a column where you can see every face. Stack them and they sink back into one.

one circle when packed · eight faces when lifted

tap a layer to name it

3 · The drawers

The hexagon book with its twelve pages tucked inside. Pull and the pages slide straight out along their arms, past the cover. Push and they slide back in.

straight out, straight back — the pages keep their crease, they just travel

tap a page to name it

4 · The pocket wheel

The whole wheel packed into a small circle that fits in a corner. Unpack it and the petals spread out to full working size. Pack it and it shrinks back into its pocket.

small and big are the same petals — unpacking is the resize

tap a petal to name it
