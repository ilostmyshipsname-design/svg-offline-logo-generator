Container upon container

A stack of containers: tin upon tin upon tin, each one holding its own layer. To see what's in a tin, you turn it — press its lid and drag it round, like unscrewing a jar. Turn it far enough and the lid swings aside and the tin's contents show. Then the tin beneath is ready to be turned. And the whole stack can pull apart into an exploded view, so you can see every container at once.

1 · The stack: turn each tin to see inside
