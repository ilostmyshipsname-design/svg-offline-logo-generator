The layered container

The container is empty space — as layered as you want. On top sits the working dial of colour effects. Pull it apart and the layers beneath slide out: more effects one layer down, more colours the layer below that. The container holds as much as you build into it.

1 · The container and its layers

A watch-style case with the colour effect dial as its top layer. Drag the outer ring to turn the effects, the inner ring to turn the colours — each re-inks the other, and the hidden layers re-ink too. Press the button to pull the layers apart.

2 · The deeper pens

Three pens that don't sit on the top layer: charcoal, spray, chalk. They live one layer down — the effects go far beyond the seven on the face of the dial.
