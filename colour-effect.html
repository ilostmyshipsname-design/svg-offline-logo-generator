The colour effect dial

Not a colour menu. Not a pen menu. One dial of colour effects — and the two rings affect each other: turn the effects and the colours re-ink themselves through the new effect; turn the colours and the effects re-ink themselves in the new colour.

1 · One dial, two rings, one thing

The outer ring is the seven effects, shown in your chosen colour. The inner ring is the ten colours, shown through your chosen effect. Turn either — the other re-inks itself. The lens shows the part; the button pins it in the container.

turn one ring and the other changes with it — that is how they effect each other

chosen: pencil · pillar red

2 · The proof

The same ten colours, once through the pencil and once through the ink. Two different families from one set of colours — the colour menu never exists on its own.

same colours, different effects — colour and effect are one thing

tap a wedge to name it
