Other circle books

Fair point — the wedged wheel is one kind and I kept showing it. Real bookbinders fold circles at least four other ways. None of these are wedges round a hub. All painted with the five effects, as the law says.

1 · The ring book

A ring of standing pages, like a round screen of cards. Press the button and the pages glide one step round the ring.

twelve upright pages round a paper ring · the cover lies flat beneath · the spine hub sits in the middle

tap a page to name it

2 · The flag book

Flags on a pleated paper spine. Press the button and the flags drop flat — press again and they stand back up.

twelve flags on a pleated spine ring · dropped flat they read like a horizon

tap a flag to name it

3 · The tunnel book

Layers with windows cut through, stacked behind each other — you look down a paper tunnel. Open it to see the layers separate.

five layers, each with a round window · the eye travels through four windows to the back layer

tap a layer to name it

4 · The lotus book

Rings of petals shingled over each other, three deep. Open it and the flower blooms outward.

outer ring ten petals · middle ring eight · inner ring six · petals slide out along their own directions

tap a petal to name it
