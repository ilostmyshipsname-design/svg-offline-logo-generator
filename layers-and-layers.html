Layers and layers

There are too many colours and too many pen types to ever fit on one wheel — so don't be afraid of using space. The container holds layers and layers, and we unpack the effects out of it, one layer at a time. Like at home: you don't keep everything on one table. You go to the drawer that holds the thing.

1 · Unpacking the container, layer by layer

The colour effects wheel is the face of the container. Unpack a layer and a drawer slides out beneath it, carrying one layer of what's inside. Another press, another drawer. The drawer faces are painted with the chosen effect — the container itself is painted. Tap any wedge to choose.

the wheel never eats the space — the layers come out to meet you

2 · The whole cabinet, seen open

All the drawers at once, so you can see what lives where. This is why nothing has to be crammed: the effects, the colours, the deeper colours — each has its own drawer, and there are more below.
