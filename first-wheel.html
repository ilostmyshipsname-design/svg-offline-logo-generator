The first wheel

A colour effects wheel, not a colour wheel. When the app opens the first time, this is on the screen: the ten colour effects round the rim, each one holding its colours attached. Tap an effect and its colours unfurl round the rim. Tap a colour to choose that colour effect. Tap the effect again and they fold back. Colours on their own live in the colour menu; pens in the pen menu — this wheel is the colour effects.

1 · The colour effects wheel

Ten effects, each in its own colour, quiet, as they arrive. Every one of them is already carrying all ten of its colours underneath. Tap an effect to open what it carries.
