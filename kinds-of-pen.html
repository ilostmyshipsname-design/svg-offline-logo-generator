Kinds of pen

Pencil, ink, watercolour, paint, marker, pastel, metallic — each is its own kind, not just a colour. That is why the menus split: type of pen, colour, effects. The effect comes with the pen.

1 · Same colour, different pens

One colour — pillar red — drawn by seven different pens. Each pen makes its own effect, so each is its own kind.

seven pens, one colour — the effect is what makes them different kinds

tap a swatch to name it

2 · The menus

Pick the type of pen, then pick the colour. The part underneath shows that pen's effect in that colour. Tap a cap to choose.

type of pen → colour → the part — the effect comes with the pen

tap a cap to choose

3 · The container of pens

Seven pens round the container, each in its own working colour. Tap to name one; the wheel turns as ever.

every pen carries its own effect and its own colour

tap a pen to name it
