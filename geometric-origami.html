Geometric origami

The circle book folded with straight creases and sharp edges instead of curves — closer to real origami, and closer to your geometric art. Every face is still one of the five effects. Four folds to try.

1 · The faceted book

Twelve straight-cut facets instead of curved wedges — crisp creases from the hub to the rim.

the cover (watercolour wash) · twelve facets, one effect each · the spine hub (metallic sheen)

tap a facet to name it

2 · The pleated ring

Accordion pleats all the way round — up, down, up, down. Open them and the ring ruffles out.

twenty-four pleats — the lit ones face you, the dark ones fold away

tap a pleat to name it

3 · The pointed star

Eight sharp points folded through the hub — each point shows two faces, so it holds twice.

each point: front face (one effect) + back face (another) · eight points = sixteen holds

tap a face to name it

4 · The folding map

Every origami starts as a flat sheet with its creases drawn. Solid lines fold up (mountains), dotted lines fold down (valleys).

the book's folding map — this flat circle becomes any of the three above, depending on which creases you choose

tap the hub to name it
