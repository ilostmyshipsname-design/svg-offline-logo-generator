Geometric circle books

Straight creases only — every edge is a fold. Four more kinds: a drum that spins, a star box that opens its points, a hexagon that opens three ways, and a ring of diamonds that loosens. All painted with the five effects.

1 · The drum

Twelve flat panels bent into a cylinder, with a paper lid. Press and the drum turns one panel.

each panel catches the light differently as it comes round — like a paper lantern

tap a panel to name it

2 · The star box

A box with eight points standing round its rim. Open the points and they lie outward like a star.

metallic walls · hatched base · eight points, one effect each

tap a part to name it

3 · The hexagon book

A circle folded flat to a hexagon, opening three ways at once — each way its own stack of pages.

three arms of four pages · creases to the six corners decide which way it opens

tap a page to name it

4 · The diamond ring

Twelve diamonds shingled round a circle, each overlapping the last like roof tiles. Loosen them and the ring breathes open.

twelve diamonds over a wash base · metallic heart at the centre

tap a diamond to name it
