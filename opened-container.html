The opened container

You asked: why spend all the space on one circle, as if there is only one circle in the container? There isn't. The container is empty space — when it opens, it can hold as many circles as it wants. The wheel keeps its size, and what it carries comes out as its own circles beside it, the way a watch carries small dials beside the hands.

1 · The container opened — a space of circles

Packed, it is one wheel. Open the container and the wheel steps aside — smaller, still whole — and its contents come out as their own circles: the colours of the chosen effect, the deeper effects, and one more circle if you ask for it. Tap any wedge to choose.
