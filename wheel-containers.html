The five containers

The wheel before any colours are pinned to it. Drag any of them round in a circle to feel them spin.

1The Astrolabe

Etched glass on blueprint blue. The + sockets fill like stained glass when colours are pinned.

2The Tray

A wooden artist's tray with a brass rim. Indented wells waiting to be filled.

3The Shutter

A camera-style iris. The blades open wider as more colours are pinned.

4The Track Ring

An open rail. Colours clip on like charms on a bracelet.

5The Cardstock Wheel

Layered paper rings. Colours soak in like marker on card.
